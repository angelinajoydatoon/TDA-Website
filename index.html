About Us

Tradean Democratic Alliance is the only legitimate partylist in ISAT U accredited by the Office of the Student Affairs And Services.

Six (6) years ago, a group of ambitious young Tradean leaders set out to establish an organization with the goal of encouraging both leadership and academic success.

The Tradean Democratic Alliance is a group of students committed to serving our peers and protecting their rights through acts of Good Faith, Justice, and Democracy.

PROJECT IGPAT: Empowering Communities Through Health and Service

Date: May 16, 2026 | Location: Barangay Sooc, Arevalo, Iloilo City

The spirit of volunteerism and youth leadership came alive as the Tradean Democratic Alliance (TDA) proudly partnered with The Outstanding Students of Iloilo Awards (TOSIA) 2026 for Project IGPAT: Integrated Guide for Personal Awareness and Transformative Health held at Barangay Sooc, Arevalo, Iloilo City on May 16, 2026.

Anchored on the advocacy of promoting health literacy, hygiene awareness, and sustainable sanitation practices, the activity gathered student leaders, volunteers, and community members in a collective effort to create a healthier and more informed community.

This partnership reflects the unwavering commitment of the youth in becoming catalysts of positive change, proving that leadership is not only about serving, but also about uplifting communities through compassion, action, and purpose.

NextGenPH Competition Entries

Year: 2026

At this year's NextGenPH competition, the Tradean Democratic Alliance presented four forward-thinking, resilient solutions designed to address energy grid failures, fuel shortages, and rural infrastructure challenges:

  • Solar-Powered Dryer & Rice Mill: An off-grid processing solution that dries unhusked palay and mills polished rice without fossil fuels or grid electricity, reducing post-harvest losses for farmers and ensuring uninterrupted grain processing for local governments during energy crises.
  • Digital Agricultural Support Platform: A digital network that eliminates the need for farmers to travel to municipal offices for subsidies and machinery advisories, optimizing scarce energy reserves and maintaining operational food supply chains during fuel shortages.
  • Offline Physical Data Network: A "sneakernet" architecture using trusted local transporters to physically carry encrypted health files on portable storage devices, keeping medical supply chains and patient tracking active without relying on internet access or additional fuel consumption.
  • Smart Cold-Storage Matching System: A digital coordination tool that connects smallholder farmers directly to nearby solar-powered cold storage facilities and local buyers, preventing post-harvest spoilage during grid blackouts and safeguarding farm incomes.
